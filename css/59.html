CSS Object-Fit and Object-Position

Object-Fit: Cover

Fills the box and crops the image.

Object-Fit: Contain

Shows the complete image inside the box.

Object-Fit: Fill

Stretches the image to fill the box.

Object-Fit: None

Displays the image at its original size.

Position: Top

Image is positioned at the top.

Position: Center

Image is positioned at the center.

Position: Bottom

Image is positioned at the bottom.
